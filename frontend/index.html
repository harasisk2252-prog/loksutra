01 / CAPTURE◉

Listen to a life.

Record a tradition with permission. Gnani speech-to-text can help turn spoken Punjabi, Hindi, and other supported languages into editable text.

02 / PRESERVE✍

Keep the meaning.

Review every word, credit the contributor, and save the record with its region, language, and context. No silent rewriting.

Follow the threads.

Find related traditions through explicit shared metadata. Inspect the original saved sentences behind every reading step.
