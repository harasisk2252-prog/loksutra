# Local demonstration

1. Open http://127.0.0.1:8000 and view the heritage illustration and archive counts.
2. Choose Explore, filter by Craft, and open a knowledge chapter.
3. Select a reading step to highlight its exact saved sentence with Source Lens.
4. View the constellation and its explicit category relationships.
5. Sign in with your existing owner password to preserve a reviewed transcript, then reopen and edit it.

Illustrative records and AI-generated artwork are labelled. Automated transcription tests use mocked provider responses; they do not establish live Gnani availability.
